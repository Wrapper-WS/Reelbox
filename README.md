# Reelbox

A video and photo gallery that plays what is already on your phone, with the smooth swipe feel of short video apps. Works fully offline.

**[Download for Android](https://github.com/Wrapper-WS/Tiktok-gallery/releases/latest/download/Reelbox.apk)**

## What it does

- Plays your own videos and photos in a vertical swipe feed
- Keeps the correct shape of every video without stretching or cropping
- Full screen landscape mode for wide videos
- Favourites, share, and delete
- Photo and video galleries with folders
- Works fully offline

## How to install

1. Download Reelbox.apk on your Android phone.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
3. Open Reelbox and allow access to your videos and photos.

## Privacy

Reelbox has no internet permission and does not collect, track, or send any data. Everything stays on your phone.
